YAPPR — CLICKABLE DATING & SOCIAL APP PROTOTYPE
================================================

Yappr is the current name for this prototype. It has NOT been checked for trademark, domain, ASIC/ABN or App Store availability. Complete those checks before investing in branding or publishing under this name.

This is a local, clickable design prototype for a proposed adult-only (18+) dating and social app. It uses fictional demo profiles and sample interactions only.

HOW TO OPEN
1. Extract this ZIP to a folder on your computer.
2. Open index.html in Microsoft Edge or Chrome.
3. Use the left sidebar (desktop) or bottom navigation (mobile) to explore Discover, Date Nights, Groups, Inbox, Meetups and Profile.
4. Open admin.html to explore the separate moderator dashboard concept.

WHAT WORKS IN THIS PROTOTYPE
- Navigation across the app sections.
- Fictional discovery profiles with like, pass and save interactions.
- Compatibility display and profile interest tags.
- Optional nearby-discovery toggle and sample privacy settings.
- Date Nights as a central workflow: choose availability windows, see fictional profiles with overlapping availability, select a date idea, and draft a local-only date proposal.
- Group joining and local group creation.
- Local-only chat message entry.
- Demo meetup planning and two-person check-in concept.
- Demo post-meet private 1–5 star feedback interaction.
- Demo profile, privacy and safety/report flows.
- Separate moderator dashboard with sample report queue, user management, content review, appeals and audit log concepts.

IMPORTANT LIMITATIONS
This is NOT a production dating app and must not be used by real people to arrange dates, share personal information or communicate.
- All profiles and conversations are fictional.
- Nothing is connected to a database or real messaging service.
- No real age or identity verification is performed.
- Nearby discovery does not access device location.
- Demo meetup codes are not secure verification codes.
- Date proposals are stored in the page only; no invitations are sent and no real person is contacted.
- Reports are not sent to moderators or emergency services.
- Admin actions do not restrict real accounts or modify real data.
- There is no authentication or access control on admin.html. Anyone with the file can open it.
- No actual subscription, payment, profile boost or push-notification service is implemented.
- No content-moderation model is connected.

BEFORE ANY REAL-USER TEST
A secure backend, proper authentication, age-assurance provider, privacy/legal review, moderation workflows, evidence storage, abuse-prevention controls, security testing and app-store compliance work are required. Do not onboard real users until those systems are implemented, tested and reviewed. An adult with legal capacity should oversee contracts, payment-provider accounts, app-store accounts, privacy obligations and any launch activity.

BRAND AND MARKET NOTE
The name Yappr has not been cleared. Formal Australian and international trademark, business-name, domain and app-store checks are still required.

We also found existing products with overlapping same-night dating/event features, including 2NIGHT and SameNight. Yappr's planned product is broader (dating, friendship, group hangouts, week-based date planning, verified meetup flow and privacy-focused social history), but those overlaps mean the product must be differentiated and validated before launch.
